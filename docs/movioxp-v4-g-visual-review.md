# MovioXP 4.0 G visual acceptance

The Android 15 emulator ran the Cinema screen with the live built-in catalogue and a seeded Continue Watching entry. Both the remote and visual instrumentation suites passed.

- Home opens with the loaded landscape backdrop, compact controls, and Continue Watching directly below the featured movie.
- The pink Explore More Action card is visible in its Home shelf.
- The standalone Action catalogue displays multiple vertical rows beneath its anchored heading.
- The held Back action opens the scaled Exit confirmation with readable text and a labelled Cancel action.
- The remote shows the D-pad, OK, Back, and the Home icon. Short Back from Action restores Explore More, and short Back on Home keeps Home open.

Physical TV, foldable hardware, and logged-in playback checks remain outstanding.
