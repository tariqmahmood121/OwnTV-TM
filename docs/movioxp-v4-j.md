# MovioXP 4.0 J — Final Cinema Refinement

- Fixed two independent blockers to featured rotation. Idle remote focus no longer suspends the timer and a provider-verified past release year can qualify a title even when the day is unknown. Full-date sorting still requires the actual date. The hero retains its artwork until a replacement is ready.
- Added a local, per-profile Cinema font choice and live preview. Montserrat is the default. The font licences are available in About.
- Updated Cinema card metadata, age indicators and rating overlays. The focused shelf card requests visibility after its artwork and metadata expand.
- Reworked the integrated keyboard and added category-aware Search recommendations. Single OK on a Cinema movie or series opens details before playback.
- Applied the supplied MovioXP wordmark to startup and Cinema login. The MovioXP launcher uses a gradient triangle.
